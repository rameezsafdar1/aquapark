[2026-10-02] AI catch-up after landing (AiManager.SetAiDynamically) - code done, NOT yet tested in Play
- Old: every AI behind the player was teleported 3-15 m behind (percent based) -> visible pop-in next to the camera.
- New: AI further behind than the hidden gap (max(35 m, camera distance + 25 m)) are moved to that gap behind the camera
  (6 m spacing between them, never pushed further back) and get a +10-15% speed boost. AI already closer are left alone.
  Boost ends when the AI is within 12 m of the player or after 15 s; the normal speed is restored. All values are inspector fields.
- Gaps use metres (level trackLength), not spline percent. Compiles with no errors.
- Still to verify in Play: stage a landing with AI far behind and check positions/speeds (staging test was started, not finished).
