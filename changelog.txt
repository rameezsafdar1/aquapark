[2026-10-06] Faster forward glide in the air, same gravity
- Request: glide forward a bit faster while in the air but keep gravity as it is.
- Cause: Locomotion.airSpeed was used for both the forward air speed and the fall acceleration (gravity -= airSpeed * dt), so raising
  it would also make the player fall faster.
- Locomotion.cs: split into glideSpeed (forward speed in the air, default 45) and airGravity (fall acceleration). airGravity has
  [FormerlySerializedAs("airSpeed")], so the scene's value (35) carried over without editing gameplay.unity. Glide 35 -> 45 m/s (~30%).
- Verified in the editor: Player shows glideSpeed 45, airGravity 35, jumpUpForce 20. Compiles. AI (AiMover) unchanged.
- Note: with the scene values a jump that drops 30 m lasts ~2 s, so the player now travels ~90 m forward (was ~70 m). The generator's
  fountain-on-spiral check (TrackPath.LandsOnLowerTrack) still assumes ~26 m (it used the script defaults) - see follow-up.

[2026-10-06] Fountain landing check uses the real jump physics
- Follow-up to the glide change: TrackPath.LandsOnLowerTrack assumed ~26 m of flight (worked out from the script defaults), but with
  the scene's values a jump carries the rider ~70-90 m, so fountains on spiral laps could overshoot the part below.
- TrackDefinition: new "Rider jump" fields matching the Player's Locomotion: riderGlideSpeed 45, riderAirGravity 35, riderJumpUp 20
  (keep in sync if Locomotion is retuned).
- TrackPath.LandsOnLowerTrack(i, glide, gravity, jumpUp): for every lower part of the track it works out the fall time for that drop
  (drop = -jumpUp*t + gravity*t^2/2), the landing spot glide*t ahead, and checks it is within 4 m of that part's centre line
  (distance to the segment, not just to control points). Drops 10-80 m. TrackGenerator passes the definition's rider values.
- Regenerated all 50 definitions + prefabs (0 failed). 75 fountains; 11 sit on loops and all 11 pass the new landing check
  (253 valid loop spots exist across the levels). Closest fountain to a start is now 552 m. 11 minor slope warnings (fountain on a
  slightly steeper slope than 14 deg).
